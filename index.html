ToySwap

Pass outgrown children's toys on to your neighbours, in person, at a scheduled community event.

ToySwap is an iPhone app. Everything changes hands face to face at a verified public venue — there is no shipping, no selling and no money in the app. This site is where you can reach us and read exactly what we do with your information.

Privacy Policy →

What we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it, and how to get it deleted.

The short version

  • Adults only. You must be 18 or over to hold an account.
  • Toys only — no photos of people. Every photo is checked automatically before anyone else can see it.
  • No money moves through the app. Nothing is for sale and no listing may name a price.
  • Your ZIP code is never shown to another member. Other people see only a rough area.
  • No advertising, no tracking, no analytics SDKs, and we never sell your data.
