The short version. We collect the least we can get away with: an email address to sign you in, a display name and handle other members see, your ZIP code, your date of birth to check you are 18, and the photos and text of the toys you list.   • No home address. There is no field for one anywhere in the app or the database.

2. No tracking, no ads, no sale of your data

ToySwap does not track you across apps or websites, does not show advertising, and contains no advertising or analytics software. We have never sold personal information and never will, and we do not "share" it for cross-context behavioural advertising as California defines that term. There is nothing to opt out of because we do not do it.

The app's privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking as false and lists no tracking domains, which is the same statement made in a form Apple can check.

3. How photos are handled

This is the part of the app that touches the most sensitive material, so it is worth spelling out.

  • Metadata is removed before upload. The photo is re-encoded on your iPhone, which discards GPS coordinates, camera details and timestamps. The file that leaves your device does not contain where it was taken.
  • Photos are stored privately, not publicly. They live in a private storage bucket and are served through short-lived signed links. There is no public URL to guess.
  • Every photo is checked automatically before anyone else can see it. We send it to Google Cloud Vision, which reports whether it appears to contain a person or explicit content. Anything that looks like it contains a person is refused. Until a photo passes, only you can see it.
  • The check is cautious on purpose. It cannot always tell a doll's face from a real one, so toys with faces are sometimes refused by mistake. That is the error we would rather make.
  • If the check fails or is uncertain, the photo stays private. A photo is never published because a check could not be completed.
  • Google processes the image to return that verdict and does not receive your name, email, ZIP or any other account information with it.

5. Where your information is stored

The app's database, sign-in service and photo storage are hosted in Canada (Amazon Web Services, ca-central-1). If you are in the United States, the United Kingdom or the European Economic Area, your information is transferred to and stored in Canada.

For members in the EEA and the UK: Canada holds an adequacy decision from the European Commission for commercial organisations, so no additional transfer mechanism is required for that leg. Where any onward transfer to the United States occurs — for example when a photo is checked by Google — it is made under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent approved mechanism.

6. Our legal grounds (UK and EU members)

If UK or EU data protection law applies to you, we rely on these grounds:

  • Performance of a contract — your account, your listings, matching you to events, and recording what changed hands. Without this the app does not function.
  • Legitimate interests — photo moderation, acting on reports, blocking abuse, keeping the service secure, and confirming accounts are held by adults. Our interest is running a service that is safe to use; we have weighed it against your interests and collect the minimum needed for it.
  • Legal obligation — keeping records of donations, and responding to lawful requests.
  • Consent — optional things you choose to add, such as a profile photo or a partner application. You may withdraw consent at any time by removing them or emailing us.

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account and profile — while your account exists. Deleted within 30 days of you asking.
  • Listings and photos — until you delete or withdraw them, or until your account is deleted. A deleted photo is removed from storage, not merely hidden.
  • Records of what changed hands — kept while both accounts exist. Deleting your account deletes these records, which means the other person's totals go down too: their count of what they received was made partly of handovers with you, and those handovers no longer exist.
  • Reports and moderation records — kept while the account they concern exists. Deleting your account deletes the reports you filed and the reports filed about you. We are telling you this rather than claiming otherwise: it means a member can clear a report against them by deleting their account. Suspensions have always worked the same way, because they are recorded on the account itself.
  • Donation records — kept for up to seven years for financial record-keeping. When you delete your account these are anonymised rather than destroyed: the link to you is removed and the transaction record remains as an untraceable line item.
  • Partner applications — kept until the account that filed them is deleted. The venue and the event an approved application produced are not affected; they belong to the area, not to you, and they stay.
  • Backups — our host keeps encrypted backups on a rolling schedule. Deleted information can persist in a backup for up to 30 days before it is cycled out.

Wherever you live, you may ask us to do any of the following, and we will do it. We will not treat you differently for asking.

  • See what we hold about you, and get a copy in a portable format.
  • Correct anything wrong. Your display name, handle, profile photo and ZIP can be changed in the app at any time.
  • Delete your account and your data.
  • Restrict or object to how we use it, including anything relying on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent for anything you gave it for.

Deletion you can do yourself, in the app, right now — Settings (the cog on the You tab) → Delete my account. For anything else, email tyswenson34@gmail.com from the address on your account, or use the support form. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm you control the account before acting, which is there to stop somebody else acting on it.

Deleting your account

You can do this yourself from inside the app, and it happens immediately. Open Settings — the cog at the top of the You tab — and choose Delete my account. You will be shown exactly what is about to go and asked to type DELETE to confirm. There is no queue, no waiting period and no undo.

It removes your account, your profile, your listings and their photographs, your attendance records, the reports you filed and the reports about you, anyone you blocked, and your record of what you brought and what you took. Photographs are deleted from storage, not merely hidden. Your sign-in credential is deleted too, so signing up again with the same email address creates a new account rather than restoring the old one.

One thing survives, and only one. If you have ever donated, the payment record is kept for financial record-keeping with the link to you removed — an untraceable line item, as described in section 7. Nothing else about you is retained.

If you cannot sign in, email tyswenson34@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days.

9. Children

ToySwap is for adults. You must be 18 or over to create an account, and the app checks your date of birth at signup and refuses anyone younger. The app is not directed to children, contains no child-directed features, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

The toys are for children; the accounts are not. If you believe a child has created an account, email tyswenson34@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Content, safety and moderation

ToySwap carries content that members write and photograph, and we do not tolerate objectionable content or abusive behaviour. Every listing, profile and event can be reported from inside the app, every member can be blocked, and we act on reports of abuse or unsafe content within 24 hours — removing content, and suspending or removing accounts where it is warranted.

Acting on a report may mean we look at the listing, photo or message being reported. We do not read your information otherwise, and we do not use it to build a profile of you.

11. How we protect it

  • Everything between the app and our servers travels over TLS, and data is encrypted at rest by our host.
  • Access rules are enforced by the database itself, on every table, rather than by the app asking nicely — so a bug in the app cannot hand one member another member's private fields.
  • Photos sit in a private bucket and are reachable only through short-lived signed links.
  • The app ships with no administrative credentials of any kind. Elevated access exists only on servers we control.
  • Your ZIP code and date of birth are readable only by you; the database enforces that with a restricted view rather than relying on the app to hide them.

No service can promise perfect security. If a breach ever affects your personal information, we will tell you and the relevant regulators as the law requires.

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. Continuing to use ToySwap after a change means you accept the updated policy.
