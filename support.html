Things you can do inside the app

Report a listing, a person or an event

Tap the flag icon in the top right of any listing, on a member's profile, or on an event page. Reports go to a queue a person reads. We act on reports of objectionable content and abusive members within 24 hours, and we remove content and suspend accounts where it is warranted.

Block someone

Open the member's profile and choose Block. You and that person will stop seeing each other's listings in both directions, and neither of you can claim anything from the other.

Delete a listing

Open the listing from My Toys and choose Delete. If you would rather keep it but hide it from everyone, choose Withdraw instead.

My photo was not posted

Every photo is checked automatically before other members can see it, and anything that looks like it contains a person is refused. The check is deliberately cautious and it cannot always tell a doll or a figure from a real face, so toys with faces are sometimes refused by mistake. Open the listing and it will tell you what the check thought it saw.

A photo gets one pass through that check and cannot be swapped out afterwards, so the fix is to delete the listing and add the toy again with a different picture — a different angle, or the toy's face turned away, usually clears it. If you think a photo was refused wrongly, email us and we will look at it ourselves.

Delete your account

Email us from the address you signed up with, or use the form above with Delete my account and my data selected. We delete your account, your listings, your photos and your records within 30 days and email you to confirm. See Your rights and choices for what is kept afterwards and why.

Who we are

ToySwap is built and run by Ty Swenson, an independent developer in Tennessee, United States. There is no support team and no phone line — email is the way to reach us, and it reaches a person.
